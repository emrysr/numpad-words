Dialpad Text Input

Ambiguous Keypad Text Entry

A predictive, T9-style text entry demo for E.161 numeric keypads - whole words from just the number keys, no full keyboard needed. It's installable as an app (look for your browser's "install"/"add to home screen" option) and keeps working offline afterward.

Long-press * for manual mode, to add custom words and symbols.

most words work out of the box - manual mode is only needed for a word the dictionary doesn't have (a name, a symbol, anything unusual).

2-9 to spell · 0 to accept · * to cycle · # to delete

wrong word? press * to cycle through alternate matches

long-press a number for its digit · long-press # to clear all

Word predictions come from the SUBTLEX-UK frequency corpus (van Heuven, Mandera, Keuleers & Brysbaert, 2014).
